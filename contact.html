Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take? ▶

We offer standard shipping (3-5 business days) for free on orders over ₦150,000. Orders under ₦150,000 have a flat ₦8,000 customs fee. Express shipping available for ₦22,500.

What is your return policy? ▶

We offer hassle-free returns within 30 days of purchase. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition with tags attached. Return shipping is free on orders over ₦75,000.

How do I find my size? ▶

Check our detailed size chart on each product page. We recommend measuring yourself and comparing to our chart. If you're between sizes, we suggest sizing up for comfort.

How should I care for my clothing? ▶

Most items are hand-wash in cold water with mild detergent. Lay flat to dry. Check the care label on each product for specific instructions. Avoid bleach and machine washing.

What payment methods do you accept? ▶

We accept card, bank transfer, and USSD payments — all processed securely through Paystack, right on our checkout page.

How can I track my order? ▶

After placing your order, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can use this to track your package in real-time.

Do you ship internationally? ▶

Right now we ship only within Nigeria. International shipping is on our roadmap — sign up for our newsletter to be notified when it launches.

Can I place a custom or bulk order? ▶

Absolutely! For custom or bulk inquiries, please contact us at support@amulreefashion.com with your details and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.
